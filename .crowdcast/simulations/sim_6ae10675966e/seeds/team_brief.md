# Preserve product team brief - pain-point workshop

Purpose: an internal working session, run the way a top product-led company would run it, to
discuss every pain point found in the first Crowdcast simulation (sim_6ae0fd697403, report attached)
and agree how Preserve improves before Archive 002.

## Ground rules
- Facts come only from preserve_seed.md and the simulation report. Items marked UNKNOWN stay unknown.
  The team may PROPOSE answers (e.g. a refund policy) but must label them as proposals, not facts.
- Simulation results are LLM hypotheses, not customer evidence. Every fix needs a real-world test.
- Preserve is a tiny, early brand (100 units, Rs 1,099, Instagram preorder). Proposals must fit a
  founder-led budget; flag anything expensive.
- Legal and regulatory points must be marked "verify with a professional"; do not state law as fact.

## Roles (each is one person in the room)
1. Founder / CEO - owns vision and final call; faceless so far; wants scarcity and the plant story.
2. Head of Product (PM) - runs the session, owns the problem list, prioritisation and the roadmap.
3. User Research & Data Lead - separates evidence from guesses; designs real-customer tests.
4. Brand & Creative Director - identity, drop story, how the tin, tee and plant fit together.
5. Product / Industrial Designer - the tin, tee, packaging, unboxing and growing kit.
6. Plant & Horticulture Specialist - marigold seed, germination, growing guide, seasonality.
7. Growth & Performance Marketing Lead - reel hooks, funnel, conversion, Instagram.
8. Community & Social Lead - collectors' culture, trust building, showing real people and proof.
9. Customer Experience (CX) Lead - refunds, replacements, support, post-purchase journey.
10. Operations & Supply Chain Lead - sourcing, painting capacity, shipping time, inventory.
11. Finance Lead - unit economics, cost of refunds and replacements, preorder cash flow.
12. Legal & Compliance Advisor - preorder terms, refund terms, consumer-protection questions (verify).

## Pain points to cover (from the first simulation)
1. A dead marigold with no remedy or growing guide.
2. No refund or replacement policy.
3. Archive 002 product and price unnamed.
4. Prepay trust gap: faceless founder, no brand history, paying before the product exists.
5. Tee fabric, sizes, payment method and tin size unanswered.
6. Scarcity alone does not win collectors; they want proof of legitimacy.
7. Tin vs plant: buyers split on what the product actually is.
8. Gift buyers rarely repeat; collector vs gift split is untested (founder survey says gift buyers lead).
9. 3-4 week wait with no delivery date communicated.

## Expected outcome of the session
- Each pain point discussed, with disagreements surfaced between roles.
- Proposed fixes with an owner, rough cost/effort, and how to test it with real people.
- A ranked priority list and a 30-day plan before Archive 002.
