# Brand Context

> Status: working draft. Filled in from the founder's seed document and two AI simulations (buyer reaction and product-team workshop). Items marked UNKNOWN were never provided. Items marked PROPOSAL came from the simulated workshop and still need the founder's confirmation and a test with real people.

## Brand
- **Name**: Preserve
- **Category**: Limited-edition collectible objects (numbered drops), sold direct to consumers in India
- **Description**: Numbered limited releases ("Archives"). Archive 001 is 100 units, each a hand-painted tin can with a T-shirt and a marigold seed, Rs 1,099, sold as a 100% preorder through Instagram.
- **Stage**: Early. Archive 001 is the first release. Archive 002 is planned about 8 weeks after Archive 001 ships (product and price UNKNOWN).
- **Website**: UNKNOWN (sells through an Instagram reel and bio link)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Two groups, with the split unproven.
  - Group A: Indian streetwear and sneaker collectors aged 19-28 who follow labels like Gully Labs and Comet and are used to scarce, numbered drops.
  - Group B: People buying as a gift for a partner or friend, mainly because they like the idea of growing a plant.
  - The founder's own survey suggests gift buyers are the main group (survey details UNKNOWN). The 60/40 collector/gift split used in the simulations was an assumption.
- **Key Problem**: Collectors want something scarce and credible. Gift buyers want a gift with a story. Both are asked to pay before the product exists, to a brand with no history.
- **Their Language**: Not yet collected from real people. Phrases that came up in the simulation (not real quotes): "a candle shop, not a drop", "paying before the product exists", "no face, no history". Collect real words from collector DMs and buyer replies.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: A numbered collectible drop that also grows. The number and the hand-painted tin make it a drop; the marigold gives it a story and a reason to keep it. PROPOSAL (workshop): "A numbered object that grows." The drop leads and the plant is the reveal inside. Not yet tested.
- **Competitors**: UNKNOWN. Gully Labs and Comet are labels the target collectors follow, so they are reference points for how drops work, not confirmed competitors.
- **Market Position**: Niche, small-batch, mid-priced (Rs 1,099). Scarcity is a core part of the positioning ("never remade, 100 means 100" is a workshop proposal that the founder adopted in the simulation).

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: PROPOSAL (not confirmed): handmade, scarce, quiet, honest, growing
- **Tone**: Casual and calm rather than loud. Mystique with proof: show hands and process, avoid shouted urgency ("LAST CHANCE") and airline-style legal wording.
- **Voice Admires**: UNKNOWN

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: PROPOSAL (from what the founder said in the workshop; confirm): the number on the tin is the identity; keep promises small and real; no invented numbers or claims; make things by hand.
- **Mission**: Working draft, not confirmed: "Make numbered objects worth keeping, and keep every promise we make about them."

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: UNKNOWN (no business target was given). Working goal from the workshop plan: launch Archive 002 only when the product, price, spec card, refund promise and a signed delivery date are all ready.
- **Key Metrics**: UNKNOWN targets. Metrics the workshop planned to track: waitlist signups, share of buyers whose marigold bloomed, repeat intent for Archive 002, dispatch date kept or missed, refund and replacement claims.
